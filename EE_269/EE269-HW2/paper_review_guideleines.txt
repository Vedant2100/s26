Paper Review Guidelines
Format
• NeurIPS format: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers
• At most 2 pages.
• Do not directly copy and paste papers' sentences. Write the review of the paper in your own words.
• Upload a single .pdf file.
Suggested Structure
• Title and your name.
• Executive summary of the paper. Brief description of the contributions and new knowledge
presented in the paper.
• Strengths. List of the strong aspects of the paper. For instance, which technical parts are
particularly interesting and novel? How does this paper mitigate the drawback of prior work?
• Weaknesses and improvements. List of weak aspects of the paper and potential approaches to
improve. For instance, what are the hidden/strong assumptions made and how to remove these
assumptions? Is the experimental setup fair and how to better design the experiments?
• Other comments. Additional thoughts and discussions on the paper. For instance, what part of the
paper do you enjoyed the most? Does the paper inspire some new ideas?
